التباعد والدوران للمجال المتجهDivergence and Curl of a Vector Field $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V},\ \nabla\times\mathbf{V}$

الـ Divergence $(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V})$ يقيس قد إيش المجال "ينفتح" ويطلع من نقطة (مصدر) أو "ينسحب" لها (مصب)، والـ Curl $(\nabla\times\mathbf{V})$ يقيس قد إيش المجال "يدوّر" حولها. الأول رقم، والثاني متجه. Divergence $(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V})$ measures how much the field spreads out of a point (a source) or gathers into it (a sink), and curl $(\nabla\times\mathbf{V})$ measures how much the field swirls around it. The first is a number, the second a vector.

∇f∇·V∇×V ← الأزرق = الانحدار (من المحاضرة 3)، الأخضر = التباعد (قياسي)، البرتقالي = الدوران (متجه)← blue = gradient (from Lecture 3), green = divergence (a scalar), orange = curl (a vector)
⚡ الزبدة في 30 ثانية⚡ The whole lecture in 30 seconds
  1. $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial v_3}{\partial z}$: كل مركبة تنشتق بمتغيرها، والنتيجة رقم.$\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial v_3}{\partial z}$: each component by its own variable; the result is a scalar.
  2. $\nabla\times\mathbf{V}$ = محدد $3\times3$ (صف $\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}$، صف المشتقات، صف المركبات)، والنتيجة متجه. انتبه لسالب $\mathbf{j}$.$\nabla\times\mathbf{V}$ is a $3\times3$ determinant ($\mathbf{i},\mathbf{j},\mathbf{k}$ row, derivatives row, components row); the result is a vector. Watch the minus on $\mathbf{j}$.
  3. دايماً: $\nabla\times(\nabla f) = \mathbf{0}$ و $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{V}) = 0$. و $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = 0$ = Solenoidal، و $\nabla\times\mathbf{V} = \mathbf{0}$ = Irrotational.Always: $\nabla\times(\nabla f) = \mathbf{0}$ and $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf{V}) = 0$. $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = 0$ means solenoidal, $\nabla\times\mathbf{V} = \mathbf{0}$ means irrotational.

التباعد (Divergence)Divergence of a vector field

ضرب قياسي بين $\nabla$ والمجالA dot product between $\nabla$ and the field

إذا المجال المتجه القابل للاشتقاق $\mathbf{V} = [v_1, v_2, v_3]$، فالـ Divergence يُكتب $\text{div}(\mathbf{V})$ أو $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V}$:If the differentiable vector field is $\mathbf{V} = [v_1, v_2, v_3]$, its divergence is written $\text{div}(\mathbf{V})$ or $\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V}$:

\[\nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\right)\cdot[v_1, v_2, v_3]\]
\[\Y{\text{div}(\mathbf{V})} = \nabla\cdot\mathbf{V} = \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v_2}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial v_3}{\partial z}\]
🔑 ملاحظة رئيسية:Key point: ناتج الـ Divergence دايماً كمية قياسية (Scalar).The divergence is always a scalar.

تباعد الانحدار (Laplacian)Divergence of a gradient (the Laplacian)

لو $f(x,y,z)$ دالة قياسية قابلة للاشتقاق، و $\nabla f = [f_x, f_y, f_z]$، فـ:If $f(x,y,z)$ is a differentiable scalar function with $\nabla f = [f_x, f_y, f_z]$, then:

\[\text{div}(\X{\nabla f}) = \nabla\cdot\nabla f = \nabla^2 f = \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2} + \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial z^2}\]

الدوران (Curl)Curl of a vector field

ضرب اتجاهي بين $\nabla$ والمجال، بالمحددA cross product between $\nabla$ and the field, via a determinant

إذا $\mathbf{V} = [v_1, v_2, v_3]$ قابلة للاشتقاق، الـ Curl يُكتب $\text{curl}(\mathbf{V})$ أو $\nabla\times\mathbf{V}$:If $\mathbf{V} = [v_1, v_2, v_3]$ is differentiable, its curl is written $\text{curl}(\mathbf{V})$ or $\nabla\times\mathbf{V}$:

\[\Z{\text{curl}(\mathbf{V})} = \nabla\times\mathbf{V} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ v_1 & v_2 & v_3 \end{vmatrix}\]

فك المحددExpanding the determinant

\[\nabla\times\mathbf{V} = \left(\frac{\partial v_3}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial v_2}{\partial z}\right)\mathbf{i} \;\Z{-}\; \left(\frac{\partial v_3}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial z}\right)\mathbf{j} + \left(\frac{\partial v_2}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial v_1}{\partial y}\right)\mathbf{k}\]
  1. مركبة $\mathbf{i}$: غطِّ عمود $\mathbf{i}$The $\mathbf{i}$ part: cover the $\mathbf{i}$ column$\partial_y v_3 - \partial_z v_2$
  2. مركبة $\mathbf{j}$: غطِّ عمود $\mathbf{j}$ وحط سالبThe $\mathbf{j}$ part: cover the $\mathbf{j}$ column and add a minus$-(\partial_x v_3 - \partial_z v_1)$
  3. مركبة $\mathbf{k}$: غطِّ عمود $\mathbf{k}$The $\mathbf{k}$ part: cover the $\mathbf{k}$ column$\partial_x v_2 - \partial_y v_1$
⚠️ جوهري:Essential: عكس الـ Divergence، ناتج الـ Curl دايماً متجه يحدد محور الدوران ومعدله.Unlike divergence, the curl is always a vector, giving the axis and rate of rotation.

Curl عند نقطةCurl at a point

curl(V)(1, 1, 1)
\[\mathbf{V} = yz\,\mathbf{i} + 3zx\,\mathbf{j} + z\,\mathbf{k} \quad \text{at } (1, 1, 1)\]

  1. اقرأ السؤالRead the question

    v₁ = yzv₂ = 3zxv₃ = z

  3. مركبة $\mathbf{j}$The $\mathbf{j}$ component لا تنسى السالبmind the minus

تحقق: $\text{div}(\text{curl}\,\mathbf{V}) = 0$Verify: $\text{div}(\text{curl}\,\mathbf{V}) = 0$

Verification
\[\mathbf{V} = x^2y\,\mathbf{i} + z\,\mathbf{j} - (x + y - z)\,\mathbf{k} = [\,x^2y,\ z,\ -x - y + z\,]\]

  1. احسب curl(V)Compute curl(V)

    \[\mathbf{i}: \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(-x-y+z) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(z) = -1 - 1 = -2\]
    \[\mathbf{j}: -\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-x-y+z) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(x^2y)\right) = -(-1 - 0) = 1\]
    \[\mathbf{k}: \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(z) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(x^2y) = 0 - x^2 = -x^2\]

  2. Divergence للناتجDivergence of the result

    \[\text{div}(\text{curl}\,\mathbf{V}) = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-2) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(1) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(-x^2)\]

    $-x^2$ ما فيها $z$، فمشتقتها بالنسبة لـ $z$ صفر.$-x^2$ has no $z$, so its $z$-derivative is zero.

تمرين صفي: div و curl لنفس المجالClasswork: div and curl of the same field

تمرين صفيclasswork(1, π, 1)
\[\mathbf{V} = xy^2\,\mathbf{i} + \cos(xy)\,\mathbf{j} - yz\,\mathbf{k} \quad \text{at } (1, \pi, 1)\]

  1. أولاً: DivergenceFirst: divergence

    \[\text{div}(\mathbf{V}) = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(xy^2) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\big(\cos(xy)\big) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(-yz) = y^2 - x\sin(xy) - y\]

  2. ثانياً: CurlSecond: curl

    \[\mathbf{i}: \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(-yz) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\big(\cos(xy)\big) = -z - 0 = -z\]
    \[\mathbf{j}: -\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(-yz) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(xy^2)\right) = -(0 - 0) = 0\]

    الأول رقم والثاني متجه ✓ و $\sin\pi = 0$ شال كل حدود الـ sin.The first is a number and the second a vector ✓ and $\sin\pi = 0$ wiped out every sine term.
